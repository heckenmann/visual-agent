# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Visual Agent project welcomes contributions and participation from people with different backgrounds and experiences. Everyone involved in the project is expected to help keep its spaces respectful, constructive, and free from harassment.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Accept constructive feedback and differing viewpoints professionally.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's private information.
- Follow the project rules and the terms of service of the services used to participate.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. This applies in project discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, use GitHub's reporting controls on the relevant account or content, or contact the repository maintainer through the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/heckenmann). Please do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. The maintainer will review reports and may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to protect the community.

## Scope

This Code applies to project spaces and to situations where someone is publicly representing Visual Agent or its community.
